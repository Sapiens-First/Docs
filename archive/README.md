# Source archive and content map

The two files in `sources/` are unchanged copies of the documents used for the handbook consolidation on 2026-09-27. They retain rough notes, duplicate content, embedded images, historical agendas, and cohort-specific instructions. They are source material, not the current handbook or a second live record.

This directory sits outside the VitePress `docs/` source root, so it is not built into the handbook site. It remains visible to anyone who can read this repository; it is not private storage.

## Decisions to clarify next

These are editorial and organizational follow-ups, not blockers to reading the draft.

- **Work schema:** How objectives relate to programs, products/services, projects, milestones, and tasks. Atlas's existing hierarchy and the notes' proposed hierarchy differ.
- **Metrics:** Which entities carry metrics; who maintains them; how targets and observations are recorded; review cadence.
- **Governance:** Adopted constitution, exact decision rights, circle-change process, and how responsibility moves beyond the current founder-centered Fellowship practice.
- **Participation:** Definitions and progression for members, Fellows, Stewards, and leadership roles; current membership terms and chapter voting rights.
- **Local groups:** Meaning of circle/hub/chapter/alliance, recognition process, and whether the source's size thresholds should be adopted.
- **People support:** Role-specific development rubrics, review process, and reporting routes when the usual contact is involved.
- **Cohort records:** Which source dates remain applicable; archived material mentions both December 1 and December 4, and different weekly planning schedules. The handbook deliberately defers to current cohort records.
- **Training and actions:** Which guides need more detailed, reviewed procedures and linked resources.
- **Reuse:** The movement source includes a CC BY-SA 4.0 notice and a condition on use of the Sapiens First name and logo. It is preserved in full. The sources do not establish a unified license for all Fellowship and newly merged material; confirm that before adding a site-wide license declaration.

## Atlas grounding

The consolidation also used the public Atlas page, `atlas-data.js`, and linked governance CSV inspected on 2026-09-27. These distinguish roles/circles from work records and separate linked responsibility from formal authority or access. They do not establish a complete metric or weekly-task schema.

Atlas remains live and authoritative for current state. No snapshot of its assignments is maintained here.
