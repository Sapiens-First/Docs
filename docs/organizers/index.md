# For Organizers

> Organizers turn shared concerns into collective action, with clear responsibilities and support from one another.

## In brief

- Organizers include **Fellows, Stewards, and Leads**. Staff are Leads.
- Each introduction is an ordered series of articles, starting with the knowledge most useful at that level.
- The handbook explains how we work; Atlas records current roles, projects, and responsibilities.

## Choose your series

- [Intro for Fellows](fellows.md) — Start with vision and strategy, then learn the handbook, Atlas, organizational structure, and practical tactics.
- [Intro for Stewards](stewards.md) — Go deeper into distributed authority, decisions, supporting people, and sustaining a circle.
- [Intro for Leads](leads.md) — Lead the work, review metrics and strategic assumptions, and support organizational learning. This series includes staff and a compensation placeholder.

## Use the handbook while you organize

Read the earlier material when you need the foundation; you can also jump to the article that answers today's question. These reading paths organize the material, rather than defining new permissions or graduation requirements.

For unfamiliar terms, use the [Glossary](../guide/glossary.md). For a specific task, use [Find anything](../find.md). Start with [How to use this handbook](../guide/index.md) to understand where to find guidance and current records.
