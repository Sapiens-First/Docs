# 2.4 Facilitation

## Summary

A good meeting has a purpose, a facilitator, and a clear record of what was decided. This page covers the facilitation practices that exist today and marks the Holacracy tactical and governance formats as still undecided. [1.4 Structure](../dna/structure.md) holds the beliefs about distributed authority; the practical meeting guide stays in [Running meetings and writing updates](../work/meetings-and-updates.md).

## 2.4.1 What facilitation is for {#what-facilitation-is-for}

Facilitation helps a group reach a result it could not reach alone. It covers purpose, preparation, participation, timekeeping, and clear outputs.

- State the purpose and prepare an agenda.
- Give someone responsibility for facilitation and someone responsibility for recording decisions.
- Make room for people who haven't spoken.
- Distinguish discussion from an actual decision.
- End with next steps, owners, and any dates that matter.

For a learning session, keep presentation time short enough for discussion and practice. For a project meeting, focus on the questions that need the group.

For a simple structure to run a check-in or general meeting, use the existing [meetings guide](../work/meetings-and-updates.md#project-check-ins).

## 2.4.2 Tactical meetings {#tactical-meetings}

A tactical meeting is a regular meeting where a circle coordinates its current work. The Holacracy format is one candidate; Sapiens First may adapt it.

::: clarify
No tactical meeting format has been adopted. Existing Fellowship check-ins and general meetings are not automatically Holacracy tactical meetings. See [2.4.4](#choosing-the-right-meeting).

## 2.4.3 Governance meetings {#governance-meetings}

A governance meeting is where a circle processes tensions and changes its roles and policies. For why the organization works this way and what is currently proposed, see [1.4.3 Governance and changing the structure](../dna/structure.md#governance-and-change); this page does not repeat it.

::: clarify
No governance meeting format or authority is established. Still needed: the adopted Holacracy constitution and version; local adaptations; who can change roles or policies; and the canonical agenda, which will live in A.2.4 once it exists. The process for changing roles and circle responsibilities in [decisions](../organization/decisions.md#when-responsibility-is-unclear) is a proposal, not adopted governance.
:::

Until then, follow the current [decision rights](../organization/decisions.md) and agreements. A change in this handbook's wording does not transfer authority.

## 2.4.4 Choosing the right meeting {#choosing-the-right-meeting}

Different meetings serve different purposes. The existing guidance covers three.

- **Project check-ins.** Discuss progress, decisions, blockers, and support. See [project check-ins](../work/meetings-and-updates.md#project-check-ins).
- **General meetings.** Connect people and build shared understanding of the vision. See [general meetings](../work/meetings-and-updates.md#general-meetings).
- **Gatherings.** Help people connect and take a next step. See [Gatherings](../practices/gatherings.md).

Tactical and governance meetings ([2.4.2](#tactical-meetings), [2.4.3](#governance-meetings)) are separate once adopted. Planning, coaching, and project meetings beyond check-ins have no guidance yet.

::: clarify
Still needed: whether planning, coaching, and other project meeting types need their own guidance, and a decision rule for choosing between meeting types. The schedule that applies to you is in your current program instructions, not here.
:::

## 2.4.5 Written updates and follow-through {#written-updates-and-follow-through}

A useful update covers:

- What you completed and learned.
- Links to the work or evidence.
- Risks, blockers, or decisions needed.
- What you plan to do next.

You don't need to narrate every task. Help the reader understand the state of the work and where they can help.

Keep agendas and notes with the current meeting records. The handbook holds the repeatable format; dated agendas are records of particular meetings. Record decisions and commitments where the people doing the work can find them, as in [when responsibility is unclear](../organization/decisions.md#when-responsibility-is-unclear). For a template, use the [project update template](../work/templates.md#project-update).

::: clarify
Still needed: which system is the authoritative record for decisions and commitments from each meeting type, and who updates it.
:::
