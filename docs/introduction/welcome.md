# 0.1 Welcome to Sapiens First

## Summary

Sapiens First brings people together to fight for democracy, prosperity, and security in the age of AI. This handbook explains the movement and helps people participate and work together. You can start with a short reading path or open the section that answers your question.

## 0.1.1 Who we are and why this handbook exists {#who-we-are}

We want AI to serve the common good. Sapiens First organizes for democratic renewal, common prosperity, and a secure future through public participation and peaceful collective action.

The handbook gives people shared language, explanations, and practical guidance. Use it to understand our approach, find a contribution, and coordinate with others. For the existing account of our purpose and approach, read [Sapiens First's mission and strategy](../strategy/index.md). The planned 1.1 Story page has not yet been written.

## 0.1.2 Who this handbook serves {#who-this-handbook-serves}

The handbook is for supporters, members, volunteers, organizers, leaders, and paid staff. Reading a page does not require holding a particular role. People can use the material that helps with their questions and responsibilities.

Existing reading paths offer these starting points:

| Understand Sapiens First and the AI Crisis | [Intro for Supporters](../supporters/index.md) |
| Explore our approach, values, and ways to participate | [Intro for Members](../members/index.md) |
| Learn organizing practices and how responsibilities fit together | [For Organizers](../organizers/index.md) |
| Understand proposed staff expectations | [3.1 Expectations](../staff/expectations.md) |

::: clarify
Preferred definitions of these audiences, and whether the Fellowship remains a distinct program, need confirmation. The existing Fellow, Steward, and Lead reading series remain available during migration. They do not settle the new role structure or define paid staff as Leads.

## 0.1.3 How to read it {#how-to-read-it}

**Start with the Summary.** It gives the main point before the details. Continue through a chapter for context, or jump to a numbered section when you need a specific answer.

**Choose a reading path.** The audience introductions link to existing articles. As new sections are published, use their chapter landing pages to find the numbered material.

**Find a specific answer.** Use site search, [Find anything](../find.md), or the [Glossary](../guide/glossary.md). For current people, projects, and priorities, consult [Atlas](https://sapiensfirst.org/atlas).
