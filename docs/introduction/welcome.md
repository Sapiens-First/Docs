# 0.1 Welcome to Sapiens First

## Summary

Sapiens First brings people together to fight for democracy, prosperity, and security in the age of AI. This handbook explains the movement and helps people participate and work together. You can start with a short reading path or open the section that answers your question.

## 0.1.1 Who we are and why this handbook exists {#who-we-are}

We want AI to serve the common good. Sapiens First organizes for democratic renewal, common prosperity, and a secure future through public participation and peaceful collective action.

The handbook gives people shared language, explanations, and practical guidance. Use it to understand our approach, find a contribution, and coordinate with others. For the account of our purpose and approach, read [1.1 Story](../dna/story.md) and [1.2 Strategy](../dna/strategy.md).

## 0.1.2 Who this handbook serves {#who-this-handbook-serves}

The handbook is for supporters, members, volunteers, organizers, leaders, and paid staff. Reading a page does not require holding a particular role. People can use the material that helps with their questions and responsibilities.

## 0.1.3 How to read it {#how-to-read-it}

**Start with the basics.** Every page opens with a Summary that gives what you need to act. Continue through a chapter for context, or jump to a numbered section when you need a specific answer. You don't need to read everything at once.

**Open the details when you need them.** Collapsible sections labelled **For role holders** or **Background** hold the extra depth.

**Choose a reading path.** The table above and [0.3.4](getting-involved.md#where-to-start-as-an-organizer) link to the numbered material. The reading paths are guides to learning, not restrictions on who may read a page.

**Look up any term.** The [Glossary](../appendices/glossary.md) explains the words we use.

**Find a specific answer.** Use site search or [Find anything](how-the-handbook-works.md#find-anything). For current people, projects, and priorities, consult [Atlas](https://sapiensfirst.org/atlas).
