# How we organize

We want people to have the clarity and support to take initiative. That means knowing what we stand for, what you're responsible for, and how decisions get made.

- [Values and expectations](values.md) set the boundaries for acting in the name of Sapiens First.
- [Ways to participate](participation.md) explain how people can get involved.
- [Roles and circles](roles-and-circles.md) explain how responsibilities are organized.
- [Making decisions](decisions.md) explains how to check authority, consult others, and raise unclear responsibilities.

Find current roles, circles, and assignments in [Atlas](https://sapiensfirst.org/atlas).
