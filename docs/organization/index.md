# How Sapiens First is organized

> How values, participation, roles, and decisions fit together, so you can take initiative and know when to check in.

## In brief

- Our values set the boundaries for anyone acting in the name of Sapiens First.
- Everyone starts somewhere; responsibility grows as you take it on, from supporter to staff.
- Roles and circles organize responsibility; [Atlas](https://sapiensfirst.org/atlas) records who currently holds each one.
- Every scope should say what you can decide, what needs consultation, and what needs approval.

## When to use this

Read this page when:

- You want an overview of how Sapiens First's people and structure fit together.
- You're deciding which page (values, participation, roles, or decisions) has the answer you need.

We want you to take initiative, and to have the clarity and support to do it well. That comes down to three questions.

**What do we stand for?** Our values set the boundaries for acting in the name of Sapiens First.

- [Values](values.md) — Core values, operating principles, leadership standards, and red lines.

**Where do you fit?** Everyone starts somewhere, and responsibility grows as you take it on.

- [Participation](participation.md) — Ways to get involved, from supporter to staff.
- [Roles and circles](roles-and-circles.md) — What roles and circles are, and how to read them in Atlas.

**How do decisions get made?** Know what you can decide, when to consult, and when to ask.

- [Decisions](decisions.md) — What you can decide, when to consult, and what needs approval.

Find current roles, circles, and assignments in [Atlas](https://sapiensfirst.org/atlas).
