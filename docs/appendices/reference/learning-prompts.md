# A.3.2 Learning prompts

## Summary

You don't need to read everything first. Give an LLM the title, link, or text of a reading and use these prompts to explore it. Treat the LLM as a sparring partner, not just a summarizer.

<!-- Source provenance: moved from the "Explore with an LLM" section of docs/strategy/resources.md (last updated 2026-09-29). The reading list itself belongs in Further Reading and Citations. -->

## A.3.2.1 Explore with an LLM {#explore-with-an-llm}

Give an LLM the title, link, or text and ask:

- "What are the 5 main ideas?"
- "What assumptions or weaknesses does this have?"
- "How would we apply this in an organization?"
- "Compare this with Holacracy, Big Organizing, or Netflix."
- "Quiz me until I understand it."

Treat the LLM as a sparring partner, not just a summarizer.

These resources offer perspectives to discuss and evaluate. Including a resource doesn't mean adopting every claim or tactic in it.
