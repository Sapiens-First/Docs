# Sapiens First field guides for organizers

> Repeatable, practical guides for organizing locally, grouped by our act, recruit, train cycle.

## In brief

- These guides follow the [act, recruit, train](../strategy/index.md#act-recruit-train) cycle: act together, recruit new people, and train others to lead.
- Act guides cover [gatherings](gatherings.md) and [peaceful actions](actions.md).
- Recruit guides cover [organizing conversations](organizing-conversations.md) and [starting a local circle](starting-a-circle.md).
- The train guide covers [training organizers](training.md).
- All organizing follows the [values and red lines](../organization/values.md) and the current campaign resources and decision rights.

## When to use this

Read this page when:

- You're new to organizing and want to find the right guide for what you're doing.
- You want to see how the act, recruit, train guides relate to each other.

**Start with one activity you can do well.** Invite others to help, learn from the experience, and share what worked.

## Guides by stage

**Act: bring people together.**

- [Gatherings](gatherings.md) — Help people connect and take a next step.
- [Peaceful actions](actions.md) — Choose and plan a peaceful action that serves the campaign.

**Recruit: invite people in.**

- [Organizing conversations](organizing-conversations.md) — Listen first, then invite people to participate.
- [Starting a local circle](starting-a-circle.md) — Bring a small local group together and share the work.

**Train: help others lead.**

- [Training organizers](training.md) — Help people learn through practice.

Whatever you're doing, follow our [values and red lines](../organization/values.md). Use the current campaign resources and decision rights for your work.
