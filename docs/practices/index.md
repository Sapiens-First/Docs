# Practical guides

Start with one activity you can do well. Invite others to help, learn from the experience, and share what worked.

- [Starting a circle](starting-a-circle.md): bring a small local group together.
- [Gatherings](gatherings.md): help people connect and take a next step.
- [Organizing conversations](organizing-conversations.md): listen and invite participation.
- [Training and facilitation](training.md): help people learn through practice.
- [Peaceful actions](actions.md): choose and plan an action that serves the campaign.

All activities should follow our [values and red lines](../organization/values.md). Use the current campaign resources and decision rights for the work you're doing.
