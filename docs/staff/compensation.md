# 3.2 Compensation

## Summary

This page names the factors that may shape staff compensation: location, role, and performance. No benchmark, currency, geography, role band, formula, or pay amount has been adopted, and none appears here. The only supported principle is that performance is assessed against the roles a person actually holds, using [3.1 Expectations](expectations.md); how scores relate to pay is undecided.

## 3.2.1 Location {#location}

Location may affect compensation, but the supplied material does not say whether or how.

::: clarify
Is location a factor at all? If so: which benchmark source is used, which geographies and currency apply, how a location is determined for someone who moves or works remotely, and what calculation method follows. None of these is decided.
:::

## 3.2.2 Role {#role}

Staff can hold Individual Contributor, Directly Responsible Individual, and Player-Coach roles, and these overlap. [3.1.4 Mixed roles and predominant role](expectations.md#mixed-roles) notes that IC, DRI, and Player-Coach are not a managerial ladder and that determining a predominant role is itself unresolved.

::: clarify
Does predominant role affect pay? If so, are there role bands or another method, how is predominant role determined, and how are expanded roles treated? No bands or method exist yet.
:::

## 3.2.3 Performance {#performance}

Performance is assessed against the rubrics in [3.1 Expectations](expectations.md). Per that page, a 3 means fully meeting expectations, and performance is kept separate from promotion. The supplied framework does not define a compensation formula.

::: clarify
Does a performance score change pay, and how? Whether scores map to pay at all, whether roles are combined into one score (the material defines none), and what a rating of 3 implies for compensation are all unresolved.
:::

## 3.2.4 Worked example {#worked-example}

The intended illustration is a person in India whose predominant role is DRI and whose performance is rated 3 out of 5. No amount is calculated, because the inputs and formula do not exist.

::: clarify
Provide the example's inputs: benchmark, currency, any location adjustment, DRI role treatment, and the effect of a 3/5 rating, together with the formula that combines them. Until then this example stays empty.
:::
