# Glossary

> Short definitions of the terms used across the Sapiens First handbook, each linking to where it's explained in full.

Not sure what a word means? This page is the canonical definitions page for the handbook: every term below is defined here in one place, in alphabetical order, and linked from wherever it first appears in the handbook.

::: source
Atlas holds current roles, role holders, circles, projects, and priorities. This page defines the words the handbook uses for them.
:::

### Accountability

One of a role's ongoing responsibilities, recorded as part of that role in Atlas. A role can have several accountabilities. See [Roles and circles](../organization/roles-and-circles.md).

### Atlas

Our live record of current governance, roles, projects, priorities, and owners — the handbook explains concepts, while [Atlas](https://sapiensfirst.org/atlas) shows who is doing what right now. See [The handbook and Atlas](index.md#the-handbook-and-atlas).

### Campaign

A concrete piece of advocacy work people can get involved in, such as a policy push or a local action series. Current campaigns and their resources are listed at [sapiensfirst.org/campaigns](https://sapiensfirst.org/campaigns). See [Peaceful actions](../practices/actions.md).

### Chapter

A larger stage of local organizing than a circle, described in source material alongside circles, hubs, and alliances. The thresholds and procedures for becoming a chapter still need confirmation before they're adopted rules. See [Local circles](../practices/starting-a-circle.md).

### Circle

A group of roles brought together around a shared purpose. A **local circle** is a small group that meets and acts together in one place. See [Roles and circles](../organization/roles-and-circles.md) and [Local circles](../practices/starting-a-circle.md).

### Decision rights

What a role or project owner can **decide** alone, where they should **consult** affected people, and which choices someone else must **approve**. See [Decisions](../organization/decisions.md).

### Domain

An area of related work that Atlas groups roles and projects under, alongside its views for roles and people. See [First steps for new members](getting-started.md).

### Fellow

Someone taking part in the Fellowship: a structured volunteer program in which they take responsibility for a project, with support. See [The Fellowship](fellowship.md).

### Fellowship

A structured volunteer program, not employment, in which each Fellow takes responsibility for a project, with support. See [The Fellowship](fellowship.md).

### Key result

How we recognize progress toward an objective, also called a success criterion. Each key result should point to a defined metric. See [How Sapiens First manages work](../work/index.md#outcomes-and-outputs).

### Member

Someone who participates in the community, helps with events, and builds connections, one of the ways to get involved with Sapiens First. See [Participation](../organization/participation.md).

### Metric

A clearly defined measure with a starting point, a target, and a date, useful when it helps someone decide better. See [Metrics](../learning/metrics.md).

### Mission, pillar, program

How Atlas organizes work: the **mission** is the overall change we work toward, and a **pillar** is a major contribution to it. A **program** groups related work around a continuing purpose. See [From mission to work](../work/index.md#from-mission-to-work).

### Objective

A change we want to achieve. See [Outcomes and outputs](../work/index.md#outcomes-and-outputs).

### Output and outcome

An **output** is something we create, like a workshop. An **outcome** is the change it leads to, like participants being able to run their first meeting.

### Input

An early-stage measure a role holder can directly influence, such as how quickly new sign-ups are contacted. See [Inputs and outcomes](../learning/metrics.md#inputs-and-outcomes).

### Product or service

Something people can use or benefit from, such as a resource center or a training offering. It can need several projects over its life.

### Project

A bounded effort to create or change something. See [Projects](../work/projects.md).

### Red lines

Things people acting in the name of Sapiens First must never do, such as threatening harm or harassing others. See [Values](../organization/values.md#red-lines).

### Role

A defined responsibility, with a purpose, accountabilities, and a scope. A person can hold several roles, and who fills a role can change. See [Roles and circles](../organization/roles-and-circles.md).

### Role holder

The person currently filling a role, as recorded in Atlas.

### Scope

What a project includes and excludes, with its objective, success criteria, constraints, and decision rights. See the [project scope template](../work/templates.md#project-scope).

### Staff

Someone working in an assigned staff capacity, with expectations set by the relevant role and their employment arrangement. See [Participation](../organization/participation.md).

### Steward

A further engagement level described in Atlas for Fellows after three months and graduation. A role with "Steward" in its title is a separate thing from this engagement level. See [Participation](../organization/participation.md).

### Supporter

Someone who stays informed and joins activities when they can, the lightest way to get involved with Sapiens First. See [Participation](../organization/participation.md).

### Tension

A gap between how things are and how they could be, raised with the responsible role or circle. See [Atlas and AI](../learning/atlas-and-ai.md).

## Reading this handbook

A possible future practice that hasn't been adopted, shown in a box with a dashed border and a yellow **Proposal** label.

### To clarify

Extra detail for Fellows, organizers, staff, and anyone else holding a role — everyone is welcome to read it.
