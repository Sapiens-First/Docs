# Sapiens First Fellowship Agreement

> The agreement every Fellow accepts before starting the Fellowship.

## In brief

- Every Fellow accepts this agreement before their Fellowship starts.
- Fellows aim to spend 5–10 hours per week on their Fellowship project during the program.
- Fellows stay in touch with their Department, join required check-ins, and uphold the Core Values and Red Lines.
- The agreement text below is reproduced exactly; [The Fellowship](fellowship.md) explains how the program works.

## The agreement

As a Sapiens First Fellow, I agree to:

* **Contribute:** Aim to spend 5–10 hours per week on my Fellowship project during the term of the program, and communicate if my availability changes.
* **Participate:** Stay in touch with my Department and participate in required check-ins.
* **Uphold our values:** Follow the Sapiens First Core Values and Red Lines outlined at [sapiensfirst.org/learn](http://sapiensfirst.org/learn) (including no bullying, harassment, or intimidation)
* **Represent Sapiens First responsibly:** Act in good faith when representing Sapiens First, and get approval before making significant commitments or public statements on behalf of the organization.
* **Protect private information:** Keep confidential information—such as passwords, personal member data, donor information, or private communications—secure.
* **Share my work:** Work I create specifically for Sapiens First during the Fellowship may be used, edited, published, and built upon by Sapiens First.
* **Hand things off:** If I leave a project, make a reasonable effort to leave behind the files, documentation, and context someone else needs to continue it.

I understand that the Fellowship is a **volunteer program**, not employment. I will not receive wages for my participation, and either I or Sapiens First may end my participation at any time.

## Acceptance

I understand that I may accept this agreement electronically. By replying to the Fellowship offer email with **“I agree”** or other words clearly indicating my acceptance, I intend to agree to and be bound by this Fellowship Agreement.
